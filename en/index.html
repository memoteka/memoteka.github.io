How to add your own meme?

1. Upload files to /cdn/assets/photos/, /videos/, /gifs/, /audios/
2. Edit the `memesLibrary` array in `/js/common.js` (add name and path)
3. Reload the page – new memes are here!
Supported formats: jpg, png, webp, mp4, webm, gif, mp3, ogg

Frequently asked

Can I add videos without seeking?
Yes, custom player has a progress bar with click & drag seeking.
Does it work on mobile?
Fully responsive – lightbox and player work great on any screen.
How to change language?
Click the flag in the navigation and pick Russian or English.
What if I want to delete a meme?
Just delete the file and its entry from the array – done.
