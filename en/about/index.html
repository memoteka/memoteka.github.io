About Memoteka

Memoteka is your personal archive of memes, photos, videos, gifs and audio. Built for those who want a beautiful and convenient way to store their media.

Features

  • 🎨 Modern design: glassmorphism navigation, smooth animations, blur, gradients.
  • 🌗 Adaptive theme: light, dark or system — choose from dropdown.
  • 🌍 Two languages: Russian and English – instant switching.
  • 🖼️ Meme gallery: photos, videos, gifs, audio – all as cards.
  • 🔍 Lightbox: click on a card – content opens fullscreen (with custom video player).
  • 🎮 Custom video player: play/mute buttons appear on hover.
  • 📱 Fully responsive: from phones to wide monitors.

All files are stored in /cdn/assets/. You can add your own by editing the array in /js/common.js.
