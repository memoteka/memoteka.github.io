Frequently Asked Questions

We've gathered the most important information about Memoteka. If you don't find your answer, write to us on Telegram.

What is Memoteka?
Memoteka is an archive of memes, photos, videos, GIFs, and audio. You can browse content, change themes and languages, and add your own files.
How do I add my own meme?
Upload the file to the appropriate folder inside /cdn/assets/ (photos, videos, gifs, audios), then edit the `memesLibrary` array in /js/common.js — add name and path. Refresh the page.
How does theme switching work?
You can choose light, dark, or system theme from the dropdown menu in the navigation. Your choice is saved in the browser.
How do I switch the language?
Click on the flag in the navigation and select Russian or English. The language is remembered across all pages.
How does the lightbox player work?
Videos and audio open in the lightbox with a custom player: play/pause buttons, volume control, progress bar with click-and-drag seeking, and time display.
How can I support the project?
Go to the Donate page. There's a form for YooMoney donations and alternative methods (Yandex.Tips, DonationAlerts, DonatePay).
Why can't I seek the video?
Wait for the player's metadata to fully load. If the issue persists, refresh the page. Our player supports seeking by clicking on the progress bar and dragging.
How do I delete a meme?
Delete the file from /cdn/assets/... and remove the corresponding entry from the `memesLibrary` array in /js/common.js. After refreshing, the meme will be gone.
Are there format restrictions?
Supported formats: jpg, png, webp, mp4, webm, gif, mp3, ogg. Other formats may not work in the lightbox or player.
Where can I get the source code?
All code is open source on GitHub: github.com/memoteka. You can fork the repository and build your own site.
